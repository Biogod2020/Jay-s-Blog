## Editorial and evidence scope
This is an analytical feature based on already published interviews, primary policy documents, and research, not newly conducted interviews. Reported names are attributed to their original outlets. The essay does not disclose the blog author's private clinical experiences or identify unreported individuals. Compressed modernity and selective institutional lag are interpretive mechanisms, not causally established findings. Status distinctions, historical data dates, limits of a cross-sectional sample, and accreditation-versus-law distinctions are retained in both editions.

The burnout chart uses 83.13% for any-degree burnout and 37.50% for moderate/severe burnout, with the same denominator of 504 valid questionnaires. The second category is a subset of the first. Data were collected April 1–20, 2025 in four southern Chinese hospitals; the paper was published August 24, 2026 (10.1371/journal.pone.0356791). These are not national prevalence estimates.
